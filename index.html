Capacity Monitoring

Compare design capacity, movement accumulation and survey-based remaining capacity.

Capacity by Dump WMT
Capacity Control Logic

Movement-based estimate = design capacity − cumulative accepted dumping tonnage. Survey-based remaining capacity = surveyed available volume × applicable density, subject to the approved design limit.

Reconciliation required

Do not add survey volume to daily movement. Reconcile the two independently to avoid double counting.
